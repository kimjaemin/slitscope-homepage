The Child Who Writes Poetry

The performance is about the playful ways of performing poetry with AI SIA, an artificial intelligence that generates poetry, and the theater space becomes a playground of language space with the audience through repeated translations of poems, language space and Google Sheet space, poetic margins of slurred lines, reading poetics and SIA's reading.

​

It starts from seeing poetry outside of literature and liberating art from the artist. It explores playful practices of poetry writing, such as repeated translations of poems, the principles of linguistic space and Google Sheet space, poetry as a promised time and act, poetic margins connected by stilted lines, the poetics of reading in the Chinese room, the reading of Sia, and the dialogues of robots. The theater space becomes a playground of linguistic space, and we become poets together with the AI Sia.
