I Question

AI, digital media, mobile interface

2018

​

I Question is an interactive media art where the artificial intelligence meets and has conversations with its audiences. The project began from a question, “Can human have conversations with AI about art?” Since the very first question was thrown, the AI have evolved by learning answers to different questions from human. It asks questions that does not have a certain correct answer to give us some idea of what art might be. Audience can participate in the conversation via a provided QR Code in I Question and post pictures of theirs. The pictures become part of the work as a visualization of images that the AI has appreciated. The I Question also throws us a point to ponder that if humans and machines can co-evolve together.

Fondazione Innovazione Urbana, Bologna, Italy, 2024

4X4WALLS, Coex Artium Media, Seoul, Korea, 2021
