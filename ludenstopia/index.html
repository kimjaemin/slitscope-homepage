Ludenstopia

AI, digital media

2021

​

The work rearranges two spaces as understood by an AI that is unaware of the historical, social, economic, and cultural implications of space, and a third space as imagined by the AI. The boundaries between individual and society, reality and imagination, survival and play are blurred, and we call it Ludenstopia - a playful world. Through the virtualization of space and virtual spatialization, the film shows both the personal space of students in their 20s and the playful space liberated from it.
